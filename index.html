Aneesh Muppidi

I'm a first-year Computer Science PhD student at Stanford, advised by Chelsea Finn. I am a Rhodes Scholar, and I previously completed my undergrad at Harvard.

▶︎ Rhodes

At Oxford I completed an MSc by Research in Engineering Science as a Rhodes Scholar, co-advised by Jakob Foerster at FLAIR and João Henriques at VGG. My thesis work was on real-time reinforcement learning: how an agent should decide how long to think when the world does not wait for it.

▶︎ Harvard

At Harvard I completed a concurrent A.B. in Computer Science and Neuroscience and S.M. in Computer Science. I was advised by Hank Yang at the Computational Robotics Lab, Samuel Gershman at the Kempner Institute, where I was a KURE fellow, and Ila Fiete at MIT. I was President of the Harvard Computational Neuroscience Society and Harvard Dharma, and I wrote a column for the Harvard Crimson. I also like film photography.
